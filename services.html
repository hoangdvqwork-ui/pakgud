Service Scope

Service Scope & Operational Breakdown

PakGud's Fan Fulfillment Service is built on three integrated pillars. Each one can stand alone or work together as a single, seamlessly coordinated operation.

Pillar 1 / Merchandise & Giftset

Merchandise & Giftset Management

"Reduce the worry around quality control and merchandise quantity errors."

Good Quality Accurate Quantity Hand-Delivered To Fans

We manage the full lifecycle from production to distribution, ensuring merchandise is tightly controlled, reaches guests on schedule, and takes the workload off the organizer's shoulders:

Warehousing & Inventory Control

Professional warehousing with full stock tracking, before, during, and after the event.

Packaging

Products are packed to standard for absolute quality protection, sorted by category and benefit tier so on-site benefit hand-off is fast and simple.

On-Site Merchandise Operations

A dedicated giftset and merchandise pick-up area, matched against ticket-tier data for fast, accurate distribution with zero duplicates or losses.

Shipping & Order Coordination

Synced order coordination from your sales platforms through to delivery at the buyer's door.

Pillar 2 / Check-In & Event Coordination

Check-In & Event Coordination

"PakGud designs and runs systems that keep movement flowing smoothly, tightly controlled from the very first touchpoint, to deliver a clean, comfortable, and safe entry experience for every guest."

Fast Check-In Clear Crowd Flow Security Control

Entry & Exit Flow Design

Zoned floor plans, signage, and clearly split lines by guest group so crowds never bottleneck at one point — with remote staff coordination that takes pressure off the check-in desk itself.

On-The-Spot Verification & Issue Handling

Direct QR scanning and reconciliation, with fast on-the-spot issue resolution to prevent check-in bottlenecks.

Wait-Time Minimization

Extra staff during peak windows and additional lines opened as needed to keep check-in speed steady.

Continuous Flow Past The Gate

Guests are guided further inside the venue, preventing clustering right after check-in that backs up the line and keeping the gate area clear.

Pillar 3 / Fan Benefits Execution

Fan Benefits Execution

"The moment every fan event is remembered for is always the direct interaction between artist and fan. We run these moments on a locked timeline, with artist safety and fan satisfaction held in equal priority."

Crowd Control Standardized Time Control Artist Security

Dedicated Interaction Zones

Hi-touch, soundcheck, and photo group areas are fully separated from the general audience flow, preventing intrusion or cross-flow interference.

Group-By-Group Timeline Coordination

Each fan group's participation time is controlled against a detailed rundown, keeping every activity on schedule without affecting the artist's timeline.

Conduct & Participation Rules

Clear rules on who can participate and in what format (1:1, 1:10...), with strict enforcement of interaction-zone conduct (filming/photography, contact, distance).

Micro-Flow Coordination

Movement organized in small groups, with controlled stop points, photo points, and exit points for a continuous handoff with zero disruption.

Security & Artist Protection

Security personnel stationed at every interaction zone, with continuous monitoring to prevent situations that breach control or violate the rules.

Pricing & Event Package Structure

Flexible Cost Structure By Scale

Every event's cost structure is built around its real scale and operational scope. PakGud offers two flexible tiers of engagement so you can optimize resources while still guaranteeing operational effectiveness.

Full Operational Tier

A complete operational solution for the entire event experience. Covers every item from check-in, crowd control, and fan benefit execution through to staffing and overall operational flow — ensuring every touchpoint in the guest journey runs in sync, seamless, and on plan.

Base Operational Tier

A core operational solution focused on the most critical touchpoints. Provides queue-control staffing at key zones: the check-in area, the fanzone area, and similar high-traffic points.
