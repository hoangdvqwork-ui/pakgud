Service Scope

Service Scope & Operational Breakdown

PakGud's Fan Fulfillment Service is built on three integrated pillars. Each one can stand alone or work together as a single, seamlessly coordinated operation.

Pillar 1 / Merch Fulfillment

Merch Fulfillment

From factory to fan's hands, we manage the full merchandise lifecycle:

Inventory Control

Professional warehousing with full stock tracking, before, during, and after the event.

Packaging (đóng gói)

Multi-layer protective packing for albums, lightsticks, and apparel, built to survive high-volume handling.

Pick-up & Distribution (phân phát cho fan)

Organized on-site booth operations that get official merch and fan-kits into fans' hands quickly, without bottlenecks.

Order Coordination

Syncing orders across your website, Shopee, and TikTok Shop, with reliable last-mile delivery for anything not claimed on-site.

Why it matters: A slow or disorganized merch line is the first thing fans complain about. We make sure it isn't yours.
Pillar 2 / Crowd & Queue Management

Crowd & Queue Management

Every fan event lives or dies by its flow. We design and run the systems that keep thousands of people moving safely and smoothly:

Entry & Exit Flow Design

Zoned floor plans and wayfinding built around your venue's actual layout.

Access Control

QR ticket scanning, wristband/pass distribution, and VIP segmentation handled by trained on-site leads.

Wait-Time Minimization

Queue structuring and staff deployment calibrated to your expected crowd density.

Scalable Security

From light-touch check-in staff at intimate showcases to full barrier systems and professional security teams at large-scale concerts.

Why it matters: Crowd mismanagement isn't just a bad look — it's a safety liability. Our queue systems are built to scale with your audience size, not improvised on the day.
Pillar 3 / Benefit Execution

Benefit Execution

The highest-stakes minutes of any fan event are the direct artist-fan interactions. We run these on a locked schedule, with artist safety and fan satisfaction held in equal priority:

Photo Sessions (Chụp ảnh)

1:1, Group Photo 1:10, and 1:50 formats, each with dedicated flow choreography.

Hi-Bye / Send-off (Chào nghệ sĩ / Tiễn nghệ sĩ)

Arrival and departure moments, managed for both energy and safety.

Hi-Touch / Handshake (Bắt tay)

High-throughput, close-contact interactions run with strict timing and physical safety protocols.

Fansign / Autograph Sessions (Xin chữ ký / Ký tặng)

Signing sessions coordinated end-to-end, from fan queueing to item hand-off.

Why it matters: These are the moments fans travel for. A benefit session that runs late, runs unsafe, or runs out of time damages the fandom relationship — we exist to make sure it never does.
