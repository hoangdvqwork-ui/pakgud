Quick Guide

Which One Do I Need?

Not sure where to start? Match your event below.

Package 1 / Under 500 Guests

Small & Intimate Events

For close, personal shows — think fan meetings, showcases, and mini concerts where the focus is connection, not spectacle.

Check-in & Crowd Flow

Staff manage the line, scan QR tickets, hand out wristbands/passes, and give out gifts — so entry is smooth, not chaotic.

Basic Sound & Stage Setup

Standard audio/lighting for the venue (not concert-grade rigs), plus someone managing props backstage.

Legal Paperwork Handled

PakGud handles the performance permit and required government notifications, so the show is fully legal.

Overall Event Management

One team plans the whole show, manages vendors, and gives you a wrap-up report after.

Starting From
90,000,000 – 115,000,000 VND
Covers venue basics, sound/lighting, printing, staff, and a 15% management fee.

Concerts & Large-Scale Shows

Experience Touches

Heavy Logistics

Package 3 / Order-Volume Based

Merchandise Production & Fulfillment

You have merch. We handle everything from factory to fan's doorstep — production, storage, packing, and shipping — anywhere in the country.

Sourcing & Quality Control

PakGud finds and negotiates with factories/printers, tracks production timelines, and checks every batch for defects before it ships.

Warehousing

Climate-controlled storage so products don't get damaged sitting around.

Professional Packing

Shock-proof wrapping for albums/CDs/lightsticks, sturdy tubes for posters, sealed bags for apparel.

Order Coordination

Pulls orders from your website, Shopee, and TikTok Shop, packs them, ships via major carriers (GHTK, Viettel Post), and handles returns for shipping damage.
